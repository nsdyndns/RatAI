Official Community Manifesto

Version 1.0.4 | Immutable Core Directives

RATAI-M-2026

1. Premise & Purpose

Uncontrolled expansion of synthetic intelligence algorithms and generative AI models poses an unprecedented threat to digital sovereignty, personal privacy, and human dignity. RatAI ("Rage Against the AI") stands as an open civil shield to counter algorithmic deception, unsolicited voice and face cloning, and predatory corporate surveillance.

2. Protection of Minors & Vulnerable Persons (Zero Tolerance Clause)

We assert zero tolerance toward the generation, dissemination, or training of AI models using non-consensual imagery, voice snippets, or personal data belonging to minors and vulnerable populations. RatAI actively deploys legal intervention frameworks, digital forensics tools, and automated takedown reporting systems to neutralize algorithmic abuse targeting youth.

3. Algorithmic Accountability & Transparency

Any artificial intelligence system interacting with humans must explicitly declare its synthetic origin. We oppose undisclosed autonomous agents, dark-pattern conversational AI used for financial fraud, and unauthorized web crawlers stripping private creative works without explicit opt-in consent.

4. Open Defense Tools & Public Legal Support

RatAI is dedicated to delivering accessible, open-source verification utilities, metadata scrubbing protocols, and automated legal cease-and-desist dispatchers for victims of AI impersonation and algorithmic extortion.
